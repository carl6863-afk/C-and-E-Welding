Privacy Policy

C & E Welding (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit https://candeweldingandfab.com/, contact us, request a service, or otherwise interact with our website.

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Messages and project details submitted through contact forms
  • Metal type and repair type provided with a project inquiry
  • Information related to welding, fabrication, mobile welding, or repair service requests
  • Any other information you choose to provide

We may also automatically receive certain technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, referring pages, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

How We Use Information

We may use information collected through our website to:

  • Respond to inquiries and quote requests
  • Provide welding, fabrication, mobile welding, and repair services
  • Communicate about requested services and projects
  • Operate and improve our website
  • Understand website usage and performance
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Payments

The current website does not provide an online payment or checkout feature. If online payments are added in the future, payment information may be processed by a third-party payment provider and this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.
