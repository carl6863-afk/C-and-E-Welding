Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an accessible and usable website.

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

C & E Welding is committed to providing a website that is accessible and usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including individuals with disabilities.

We work to improve the accessibility and usability of https://candeweldingandfab.com/ and aim to follow generally recognized accessibility practices, including relevant guidance from the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).

Accessibility is an ongoing process, and website technologies, content, and third-party integrations may change over time.

If you experience difficulty accessing information, using a feature, completing a form, or navigating any portion of this website, please contact us.

When contacting us, please describe the page or feature you were attempting to access and the difficulty you experienced. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the requested information or service through an accessible alternative where appropriate.
